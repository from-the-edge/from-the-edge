Shubhanginee Singh, PhD

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the School of Humanities, Tallinn University, where I am working on the project Memory, Everyday Bordering Experiences and Tourism. This project is supported by the Mobilitas Incoming Postdoctoral Grant awarded by the Estonian Research Council (ETAG) within the framework of the Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowship (MSCA-PF) programme.

Previously, I worked as an Assistant Professor at National Law University Odisha, with academic specialisation in citizenship and border studies in Northeast India. My work focussed on identity and resource politics, infrastructure development, and governance issues. I have also served as a Visiting Assistant Professor at Dr. B.R. Ambedkar National Law University.

I earned my PhD in Political Science from Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi, in 2024. My thesis was titled "Governing Borderlands: State, Communities, and Border Management in Arunachal Pradesh," advised by Prof. Anupama Roy. I also hold an M.Phil from JNU and a Masters in Political Science from the University of Delhi.
